Aloo Paratha Recipe

Dough Ingredients:

  • 2 cups whole wheat flour
  • Water, as needed
  • Salt to taste

Stuffing Ingredients:

  • 2 large potatoes, boiled and mashed
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 green chilies, finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 tablespoon fresh coriander leaves, chopped
  • Salt to taste

Instructions:

  1. In a large bowl, mix whole wheat flour, salt, and enough water to make a soft dough. Cover and set aside.
  2. For the stuffing, heat oil in a pan and add cumin seeds. Let them splutter.
  3. Add chopped onions and green chilies. Sauté until onions are translucent.
  4. Add mashed potatoes, turmeric powder, red chili powder, garam masala, chopped coriander leaves, and salt. Mix well.
  5. Divide the dough into equal-sized balls. Roll each ball into a small disc.
  6. Place a portion of the potato stuffing in the center of the disc and seal the edges by bringing them together.
  7. Flatten the stuffed ball and roll it gently into a paratha.
  8. Heat a tawa or griddle and cook the paratha on both sides until golden brown, using oil or ghee as needed.
  9. Repeat the process for the remaining dough and stuffing.
  10. Serve hot with yogurt, pickle, or any chutney of your choice.
