Dal Makhani Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup whole black lentils (sabut urad dal)
  • 1/4 cup red kidney beans (rajma)
  • 4 cups water for pressure cooking
  • 2 tablespoons ghee or butter
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 green chili, slit
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 1/4 cup cream
  • 2 tablespoons chopped coriander leaves for garnish

Instructions:

  1. Wash and soak the whole black lentils and red kidney beans in water overnight or for at least 8 hours.
  2. Drain the soaked lentils and beans. Rinse them thoroughly.
  3. In a pressure cooker, add the lentils, beans, and 4 cups of water. Pressure cook for about 20-25 minutes or until they are soft and well cooked.
  4. In a separate pan, heat ghee or butter. Add cumin seeds and let them splutter.
  5. Add chopped onions and sauté until they turn golden brown.
  6. Add ginger-garlic paste and sauté until the raw smell disappears.
  7. Add tomato puree, slit green chili, turmeric powder, red chili powder, garam masala, and salt. Cook until the oil separates from the masala.
  8. Add the cooked lentils and beans to the masala. Mix well and simmer for 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  9. Add cream and mix well. Simmer for another 5 minutes.
  10. Garnish with chopped coriander leaves.
  11. Serve hot with rice, naan, or roti.
