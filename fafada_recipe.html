Fafda Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups gram flour (besan)
  • 1/4 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon carom seeds (ajwain)
  • 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tablespoon hot oil
  • Water as needed
  • Oil for frying

Instructions:

  1. In a mixing bowl, combine gram flour, turmeric powder, carom seeds, baking soda, salt, and hot oil.
  2. Add water gradually to form a smooth, pliable dough.
  3. Divide the dough into small portions and roll each portion into a thin, flat strip (fafda).
  4. Heat oil in a deep frying pan over medium heat.
  5. Gently slide the rolled fafdas into the hot oil and fry until golden brown and crisp.
  6. Remove the fafdas from the oil and drain excess oil on paper towels.
  7. Serve hot fafdas with green chutney or tamarind chutney.
