Thepla Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups whole wheat flour
  • 1 cup fenugreek leaves (fresh or dried), finely chopped
  • 2 tablespoons yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chili powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed
  • Oil or ghee for cooking

Instructions:

  1. In a large mixing bowl, combine whole wheat flour, fenugreek leaves, yogurt, oil, cumin seeds, turmeric powder, red chili powder, and salt.
  2. Knead the mixture into a soft dough using water as needed. Let the dough rest for about 15-20 minutes.
  3. Divide the dough into small portions and roll each portion into a thin, round disc (thepla).
  4. Heat a griddle or tawa over medium heat and cook each thepla with a little oil or ghee until golden brown spots appear on both sides.
  5. Repeat the process with the remaining dough portions.
  6. Serve hot theplas with yogurt, pickle, or any chutney of your choice.
