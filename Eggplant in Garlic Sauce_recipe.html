Eggplant in Garlic Sauce Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 2 medium-sized eggplants, cut into cubes
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon ginger, minced
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon hoisin sauce
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch
  • 1/4 cup vegetable broth
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 green onions, chopped (for garnish)
  • Sesame seeds (for garnish)

Instructions:

  1. In a small bowl, mix soy sauce, rice vinegar, hoisin sauce, cornstarch, and vegetable broth to make the sauce.
  2. Heat vegetable oil in a large pan or wok over medium heat.
  3. Add minced garlic, ginger, and red pepper flakes (if using). Sauté for 1 minute.
  4. Add eggplant cubes to the pan and stir-fry for 8-10 minutes until they are softened.
  5. Pour the sauce over the eggplant and stir well to coat.
  6. Cook for another 2-3 minutes until the sauce thickens and coats the eggplant.
  7. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  8. Garnish with chopped green onions and sesame seeds.
  9. Serve hot as a main dish with steamed rice.
