Veg Arancini Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups cooked Arborio rice
  • 1 cup marinara sauce
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1 cup mozzarella cheese, diced into small cubes
  • Vegetable oil for frying
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. In a large mixing bowl, combine cooked Arborio rice, marinara sauce, grated Parmesan cheese, chopped fresh parsley, salt, and pepper. Mix well to combine.
  2. In a separate small bowl, mix breadcrumbs with a pinch of salt and pepper.
  3. In another small bowl, mix all-purpose flour with water to create a slurry.
  4. Take a small portion of the rice mixture and flatten it in your palm. Place a cube of mozzarella cheese in the center and wrap the rice mixture around it, forming a ball.
  5. Dip each rice ball in the flour slurry, then coat it evenly with breadcrumbs.
  6. Heat vegetable oil in a deep pan or skillet over medium heat.
  7. Carefully place the coated rice balls in the hot oil and fry until golden brown and crispy, about 3-4 minutes per side.
  8. Remove the arancini from the oil and drain on paper towels.
  9. Serve hot with marinara sauce for dipping.
