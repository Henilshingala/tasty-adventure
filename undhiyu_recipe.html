Undhiyu Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup fresh pigeon peas (tuvar dana)
  • 1 cup fresh beans, chopped
  • 1 cup fresh purple yam (kand), peeled and chopped
  • 1 cup fresh sweet potato, peeled and chopped
  • 1 cup fresh green garlic, chopped
  • 1 cup fresh fenugreek leaves (methi), chopped
  • 1 cup fresh coriander leaves, chopped
  • 1 cup fresh grated coconut
  • 1 tablespoon ginger paste
  • 1 tablespoon garlic paste
  • 2-3 green chilies, chopped
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 2 teaspoons coriander powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin powder
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Salt to taste
  • 4 tablespoons oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon asafoetida (hing)

Instructions:

  1. In a large mixing bowl, combine pigeon peas, beans, purple yam, sweet potato, green garlic, fenugreek leaves, coriander leaves, grated coconut, ginger paste, garlic paste, green chilies, turmeric powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, sugar, lemon juice, and salt. Mix well.
  2. Heat oil in a pressure cooker. Add asafoetida.
  3. Add the prepared vegetable mixture to the pressure cooker. Mix well.
  4. Cover the pressure cooker and cook for about 3-4 whistles or until the vegetables are tender.
  5. Once the pressure releases, carefully open the pressure cooker.
  6. Stir the undhiyu gently, ensuring all the flavors are well combined.
  7. Transfer the undhiyu to a serving dish.
  8. Serve hot undhiyu with puri, roti, or rice.
