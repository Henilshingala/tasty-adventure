Baingan Bharta Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 2 large eggplants (baingan)
  • 2 tomatoes, finely chopped
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 green chilies, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1-inch ginger, grated
  • 1/4 cup cilantro (coriander leaves), chopped
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • Salt to taste

Instructions:

  1. Roast the eggplants over an open flame or in the oven until the skin is charred and the flesh is soft. Allow them to cool.
  2. Once cooled, remove the charred skin and mash the flesh with a fork or potato masher. Set aside.
  3. Heat oil in a pan and add cumin seeds. Let them splutter.
  4. Add chopped onions, garlic, and ginger. Sauté until onions are translucent.
  5. Add chopped tomatoes and green chilies. Cook until tomatoes are soft and mushy.
  6. Add turmeric powder, red chili powder, and salt. Mix well.
  7. Now add the mashed eggplant to the pan. Mix everything together.
  8. Cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  9. Add garam masala and chopped cilantro. Mix well and cook for another 2 minutes.
  10. Remove from heat and serve hot with roti or rice.
