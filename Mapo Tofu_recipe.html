Mapo Tofu Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1 block (14 oz) firm tofu, cubed
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon ginger, minced
  • 2 tablespoons fermented black beans, rinsed and minced
  • 2 green onions, chopped (white and green parts separated)
  • 1 tablespoon Sichuan peppercorns, toasted and ground
  • 2 tablespoons chili bean paste
  • 1 cup vegetable broth
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch mixed with 2 tablespoons water
  • Salt to taste

Instructions:

  1. Heat vegetable oil in a large pan or wok over medium heat.
  2. Add minced garlic, minced ginger, fermented black beans, and white parts of green onions. Sauté for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
  3. Add ground Sichuan peppercorns and chili bean paste to the pan. Stir-fry for another minute.
  4. Add cubed tofu to the pan. Gently stir to coat tofu with the sauce.
  5. Pour in vegetable broth and soy sauce. Add sugar. Stir gently to combine.
  6. Simmer for 5-7 minutes until tofu is heated through and the sauce has thickened slightly.
  7. Taste and adjust seasoning with salt if needed.
  8. Stir in the cornstarch mixture to thicken the sauce further. Cook for another minute until the sauce is glossy and thick.
  9. Remove from heat and garnish with chopped green onions (green parts).
  10. Serve hot with steamed rice.
