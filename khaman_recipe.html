Khaman Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup gram flour (besan)
  • 1/4 cup semolina (rava)
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon ginger-green chili paste
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon fruit salt (eno)
  • 3/4 cup water
  • Salt to taste
  • For tempering:
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 1 teaspoon sesame seeds
  • 2-3 green chilies, chopped
  • A few curry leaves
  • 2 tablespoons chopped coriander leaves

Instructions:

  1. In a mixing bowl, combine gram flour, semolina, sugar, lemon juice, ginger-green chili paste, turmeric powder, fruit salt, water, and salt. Mix well to form a smooth batter.
  2. Grease a steaming dish or plate.
  3. Pour the batter into the greased dish and spread it evenly.
  4. Steam the batter for about 15-20 minutes until cooked through.
  5. Once cooked, remove the steamed khaman from the steamer and let it cool slightly.
  6. For tempering, heat oil in a small pan.
  7. Add mustard seeds and let them splutter.
  8. Then add sesame seeds, chopped green chilies, and curry leaves. Sauté for a few seconds.
  9. Pour this tempering over the steamed khaman.
  10. Garnish with chopped coriander leaves.
  11. Cut into squares or diamonds and serve khaman with green chutney or tamarind chutney.
