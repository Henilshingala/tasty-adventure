Chole Bhature Recipe

Chole Ingredients:

  • 1 cup chickpeas (chole), soaked overnight
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, finely chopped
  • 2 green chilies, chopped
  • 1 inch ginger, grated
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves for garnish

Bhature Ingredients:

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour (maida)
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
  • Salt to taste
  • Water, as needed
  • Oil for frying

Instructions:

  1. Pressure cook soaked chickpeas with salt until soft. Set aside.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and add cumin seeds. Let them splutter.
  3. Add chopped onions, garlic, ginger, and green chilies. Sauté until onions are golden brown.
  4. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until they turn mushy.
  5. Add coriander powder, turmeric powder, red chili powder, and salt. Mix well.
  6. Add cooked chickpeas along with 1 cup of water. Bring to a boil and simmer for 10-15 minutes.
  7. Add garam masala and cook for another 5 minutes. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves.
  8. For bhature, mix all-purpose flour, yogurt, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a bowl.
  9. Add water gradually to form a soft dough. Cover and let it rest for 2 hours.
  10. Divide the dough into small balls and roll them into oval or round shapes.
  11. Heat oil in a deep pan for frying. Fry the bhaturas until they puff up and turn golden brown on both sides.
  12. Serve hot chole with bhature and enjoy!
