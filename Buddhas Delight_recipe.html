Buddha's Delight Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup sliced carrots
  • 1 cup sliced bell peppers
  • 1 cup sliced mushrooms
  • 1 cup sliced tofu
  • 1 cup sliced cabbage
  • 1 cup sliced bamboo shoots
  • 1 cup sliced water chestnuts
  • 1 cup sliced snow peas
  • 1 cup sliced bok choy
  • 1 cup sliced bean sprouts
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons hoisin sauce
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon ginger, minced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil for cooking

Instructions:

  1. Heat vegetable oil in a large pan or wok over medium-high heat.
  2. Add garlic and ginger, sauté for 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Add tofu and stir-fry until lightly browned.
  4. Add all the sliced vegetables and stir-fry for 5-7 minutes until they are tender-crisp.
  5. In a small bowl, mix soy sauce, hoisin sauce, and sesame oil. Pour over the vegetables and tofu.
  6. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Stir well to combine.
  7. Cook for another 2-3 minutes until everything is heated through.
  8. Serve hot as a main dish or with steamed rice.
Dhokla Recipe

Dhokla Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup gram flour (besan)
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 1/4 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon ginger-green chili paste
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon eno (fruit salt)
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 1 teaspoon sesame seeds
  • 2 green chilies, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons chopped coriander leaves
  • Grated coconut for garnishing (optional)

Instructions:

  1. In a bowl, mix gram flour, yogurt, turmeric powder, ginger-green chili paste, sugar, lemon juice, and salt.
  2. Add a little water to make a smooth batter.
  3. Grease a steaming dish and pour the batter into it.
  4. Steam the batter for about 15-20 minutes until a toothpick inserted comes out clean.
  5. Let it cool for a few minutes, then cut into squares or diamonds.
  6. Heat oil in a small pan and add mustard seeds.
  7. Once they crackle, add sesame seeds and green chilies. Sauté for a few seconds.
  8. Pour this tempering over the dhokla.
  9. Garnish with chopped coriander leaves and grated coconut.
  10. Serve dhokla with green chutney or tamarind chutney.
