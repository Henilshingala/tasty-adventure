Veg Gnocchi alla Sorrentina Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1 lb (450g) potato gnocchi
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil leaves, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add minced garlic and sauté until fragrant.
  3. Add marinara sauce to the skillet and simmer for a few minutes.
  4. Cook gnocchi in a large pot of boiling salted water according to package instructions. Drain well.
  5. Transfer the cooked gnocchi to the skillet with the marinara sauce. Stir to coat evenly.
  6. Stir in chopped basil leaves and season with salt and pepper to taste.
  7. Transfer the gnocchi and sauce mixture to a baking dish. Spread it out evenly.
  8. Sprinkle shredded mozzarella cheese and grated Parmesan cheese over the top.
  9. Bake in the preheated oven for 15-20 minutes or until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
  10. Remove from the oven and let it cool for a few minutes before serving.
  11. Garnish with additional fresh basil leaves if desired and serve hot.
