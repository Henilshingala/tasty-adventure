Vegetable Spring Rolls Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 10 spring roll wrappers
  • 2 cups shredded cabbage
  • 1 cup shredded carrots
  • 1 cup bean sprouts
  • 1/2 cup sliced bell peppers
  • 1/2 cup sliced mushrooms
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon hoisin sauce
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 1 teaspoon cornstarch mixed with 1 tablespoon water
  • Vegetable oil for frying
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. In a large pan or wok, heat sesame oil over medium heat.
  2. Add minced garlic and sauté for 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Add shredded cabbage, shredded carrots, sliced bell peppers, and sliced mushrooms to the pan. Stir-fry for 3-4 minutes until vegetables are slightly softened.
  4. Add bean sprouts to the pan. Stir-fry for another 2 minutes.
  5. In a small bowl, mix soy sauce, hoisin sauce, and cornstarch mixture. Pour over the vegetables. Stir well to combine. Cook for another 2 minutes until the sauce thickens slightly.
  6. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Remove from heat and let the filling cool completely.
  7. Place a spring roll wrapper on a clean surface. Spoon 2-3 tablespoons of the vegetable filling onto the wrapper.
  8. Roll up the wrapper, folding the sides over the filling and rolling tightly. Seal the edge with a little water.
  9. Heat vegetable oil in a deep pan or skillet over medium-high heat.
  10. Carefully place the spring rolls in the hot oil and fry until golden brown and crispy, about 3-4 minutes per side.
  11. Remove the spring rolls from the oil and drain on paper towels.
  12. Serve hot with dipping sauce of your choice.
