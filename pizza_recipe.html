Veg Pizza Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1 pre-made pizza dough
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup sliced bell peppers
  • 1/2 cup sliced mushrooms
  • 1/4 cup sliced black olives
  • 1/4 cup sliced red onions
  • 1/4 cup sliced tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup corn kernels
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh basil
  • 1/4 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a lightly floured surface to your desired thickness.
  3. Transfer the rolled-out dough to a pizza pan or baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
  4. Spread pizza sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a small border around the edges.
  5. Sprinkle shredded mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
  6. Arrange sliced bell peppers, mushrooms, black olives, red onions, tomatoes, and corn kernels on top of the cheese.
  7. Season with dried oregano, chopped fresh basil, red pepper flakes (if using), salt, and pepper.
  8. Bake in the preheated oven for 15-20 minutes or until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbly and melted.
  9. Remove from the oven and let it cool for a few minutes before slicing.
  10. Serve hot and enjoy your homemade veg pizza!
