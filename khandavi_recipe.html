Khandavi Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup gram flour (besan)
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 cups water
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon ginger paste
  • 1/2 teaspoon green chili paste
  • 1/2 teaspoon asafoetida (hing)
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 1 teaspoon sesame seeds
  • 2-3 green chilies, chopped
  • A few curry leaves
  • 2 tablespoons chopped coriander leaves

Instructions:

  1. In a bowl, mix gram flour, yogurt, water, turmeric powder, ginger paste, green chili paste, asafoetida, and salt.
  2. Heat this mixture in a non-stick pan over medium heat, stirring continuously to prevent lumps.
  3. Cook until the mixture thickens and leaves the sides of the pan.
  4. Quickly spread a thin layer of this mixture onto a clean, greased surface.
  5. Allow it to cool slightly, then cut into strips.
  6. Roll each strip gently to form a roll and place on a serving dish.
  7. Heat oil in a small pan and add mustard seeds.
  8. Once they crackle, add sesame seeds, green chilies, and curry leaves. Sauté for a few seconds.
  9. Pour this tempering over the khandavi rolls.
  10. Garnish with chopped coriander leaves.
  11. Serve khandavi as a snack or appetizer.
