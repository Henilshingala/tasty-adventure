Sarson Da Saag and Makki Di Roti Recipe

Sarson Da Saag Ingredients:

  • 1 bunch mustard greens (sarson), washed and chopped
  • 1 bunch spinach, washed and chopped
  • 2 green chilies, chopped
  • 1 inch ginger, grated
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 tablespoon cornmeal (makki ka atta)
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tablespoons ghee or butter

Makki Di Roti Ingredients:

  • 2 cups maize flour (makki ka atta)
  • Warm water, as needed
  • Salt to taste
  • Ghee or butter, for serving

Instructions:

  1. In a large pot, combine mustard greens, spinach, green chilies, ginger, garlic, onion, tomatoes, turmeric powder, red chili powder, cornmeal, and salt.
  2. Add water as needed to cover the ingredients and bring to a boil.
  3. Reduce heat and simmer for 30-40 minutes until the greens are tender.
  4. Using an immersion blender or potato masher, roughly blend the cooked greens until desired consistency is reached.
  5. Heat ghee or butter in a separate pan and add the blended greens. Cook for another 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  6. For Makki Di Roti, mix maize flour, warm water, and salt in a bowl to form a soft dough.
  7. Divide the dough into small balls and flatten each ball into a round disc using your palms.
  8. Cook the rotis on a hot griddle or tawa until golden brown spots appear on both sides, brushing with ghee or butter.
  9. Serve hot Sarson Da Saag with Makki Di Roti and enjoy!
